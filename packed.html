Hey. I'm Clyde. I live in your text messages.
Here's what I've noticed: meeting people is hard. Not because people are hard — people are great. It's the friction. The group chats that go nowhere. The "we should hang out sometime" that quietly expires. The activation energy it takes to actually be somewhere, with someone, doing something real.
I remove all of that.
Every week, hosts across Boston open their doors for card game nights. I know who's looking for a game. I know who lives nearby, what vibe they're after, whether they prefer somewhere dry, whether they're okay with a dog in the room. I put the right people in the right room.
You get a text. You show up. You play cards.
That's it. No app to download. No profile pic. No swiping.
No friction.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025. We take your data seriously, especially because this involves your phone number.

What We Collect

When you text in, we collect your phone number, the contents of your conversations with Clyde, and the preference information you share during onboarding — including your name, neighborhood, social preferences, and any accessibility needs you choose to disclose.

How We Use It

Your data is used exclusively to match you with compatible game nights and communicate with you about those games. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it, share it, or transfer it to any third party except the services listed below that are necessary to run the platform.

Third-Party Services

Firebase (Google) — stores your profile and conversation history. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

OpenRouter / Anthropic — processes conversation content to generate Clyde's responses. Messages are sent to their APIs to produce replies. See their respective privacy policies for data retention details.

We keep your profile and conversation history for as long as you are an active user. If you opt out (text STOP), your data is retained but no further messages are sent. To request full deletion of your data, text DELETE or reach out directly.
